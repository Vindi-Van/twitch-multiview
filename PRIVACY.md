# Privacy

Multiview has no servers, analytics or ads of its own, and collects nothing about you.

## What stays on your TV

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your Twitch sign-in (access and refresh tokens, login name, user ID) | The app's private storage | To show who you follow and to search. Sign out from Account to delete it. |
| Twitch's website cookies, if you sign in to the video player | The WebView's cookie store | So the players and chat know you (Turbo, subscriptions, badges). Sign out deletes them. |
| Your lineup, layout, chat choice and last search | The page's local storage | So Multiview opens the way you left it. |

Nothing is backed up or transferred to other devices: Android backup is turned off.

## Who the app talks to

| Server | For |
|---|---|
| `player.twitch.tv`, `www.twitch.tv`, Twitch's video servers | The embedded players and chat, which are Twitch's own. Twitch's [privacy notice](https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/) applies to them. |
| `id.twitch.tv`, `api.twitch.tv` | Signing in, and the lists of followed, searched and top live channels |
| `static-cdn.jtvnw.net` | Stream preview images |
| `fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com` | The interface fonts (Figtree and JetBrains Mono) |
