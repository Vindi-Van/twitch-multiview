## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-19

First public release.

### Added

- **Up to four Twitch streams at once,** with the sound from the one you choose:
  - one stream fills the screen;
  - two sit side by side;
  - three share a 2×2 grid;
  - four open in focus (the one you hear is big) or as four equal squares.
- Sign-in with a code shown on the TV (and a QR code), approved on your phone at twitch.tv/activate.
- Following (your followed channels that are live), Search and Top live, with a lineup tray for picking up to four streams.
- Per-stream chat, shown in free space next to the video.
- Optional sign-in to Twitch's player (twitch.tv), so Turbo and subscriptions apply.
- A hold-OK menu on any stream: listen, show or hide chat, swap the channel, remove it, switch layout.
- A watchdog that resumes paused players and reloads stalled ones.
